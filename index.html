Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 07, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SAH Labs ("we", "our", or "us") manages information in connection with the mobile application Arrow Away: Puzzle Escape (the "App").

The App is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. We do not directly collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, or account credentials).

The App integrates third-party services that may collect automated diagnostic or anonymous advertising data to serve advertisements (Banner and Rewarded Ads). We use:

3. Children's Privacy

The App is targeted for a general audience aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate actions.

4. Data Security

All interactions between the App and third-party advertising SDKs are encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not operate external database servers that store personal user records.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any updates will become effective immediately upon posting on this page.
